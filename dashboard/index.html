How the implied value is worked out

implied = TVL × compRatio × sizeFactor × growthMult × maturityMult × chainMult

compRatio is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live-token protocols in the same category (at least 3 of them); where a category has too few live comparables the board falls back to the global median across all live-token protocols — those categories are listed below. sizeFactor damps giants: (TVL ÷ $100M)−0.15, clamped to 0.5–1.3. growthMult adds 60% of the 7-day TVL change (clamped −40%…+60%). maturityMult grows with how long the protocol has run with no token recorded, from 0.65 toward 1.00 over three years. chainMult adds 2% per extra chain, up to 10%.

An implied value is this radar's model, not a price and not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. “Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no token for the protocol — it is not proof the protocol has none. Live-token protocols are shown separately at their market caps and never enter the ranking.
